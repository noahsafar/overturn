CHIEF COMPLAINT
Changing pigmented lesion, right shoulder.

HPI
16-year-old female presents with mother for evaluation of a pigmented
lesion on the right upper shoulder, first noticed by mother approximately
4 months ago, with reported increase in size and slight color variability.
Patient denies pruritus or bleeding. Family history significant for
melanoma (paternal grandfather, age 64). Patient has Fitzpatrick II skin.

EXAMINATION
Focused skin exam of head/neck/upper trunk. Lesion of interest:
right superior trapezius region, 7 mm x 5 mm asymmetric pigmented
macule, irregular border, two shades of brown with central darkening.
Dermoscopy: atypical network with focal disorganization.
ABCDE: positive for asymmetry, border irregularity, color variation,
diameter > 6 mm. No palpable axillary lymphadenopathy.

CONSENT
Risks/benefits/alternatives discussed with patient and mother
(legal guardian present). Informed consent obtained for shave biopsy.

PROCEDURE (CPT 11102 — tangential biopsy, single lesion)
- Site marked. Skin prepped with chlorhexidine.
- Local anesthesia: 1.0 mL lidocaine 1% with epinephrine.
- Tangential (shave) biopsy performed using a #15 blade. Lesion
  removed in toto to depth of mid-reticular dermis.
- Hemostasis: aluminum chloride 35%.
- Dressing applied. Specimen submitted to dermatopathology
  (Westbrook Dermpath, accession #WD-DP-2024-04471) for histologic
  evaluation. Differential includes dysplastic nevus vs early melanoma.
- Patient tolerated procedure well. No complications.

ASSESSMENT
1. Pigmented lesion, right shoulder, clinically atypical (D48.5).
2. Family history of melanoma (Z80.8).
3. Pending pathology results.

PLAN
- Pathology pending; results in 5-7 business days.
- Wound care instructions provided in writing.
- Return for results review and further management.
- Total body skin exam offered for surveillance given family history;
  scheduled for 6 weeks (separate visit).
