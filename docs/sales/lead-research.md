# Lead research — where to find them

You need 30 leads by end of day 1. Here's the menu, ordered by how fast they
produce a name with a real email address.

## Tier 1: Yale leverage (warm intros — start here)

Yale affiliation is your single biggest sales asset. Practice owners will
take a 20-min meeting with a Yale student that they'd reject from a generic
founder cold-email. Use it deliberately, not embarrassedly.

### Yale offices that exist explicitly to do this for you

- **Yale Ventures** (yale.edu/ventures) — the university's tech transfer + startup support office. They have an industry-engagement team that does intros for Yale-affiliated founders. Email startups@yale.edu, mention you're a student building in healthcare RCM, ask if anyone has done outreach to CT independent practices. 1-week turnaround typical.
- **Tsai CITY** (tsai.yale.edu) — innovation hub. They run programming for student founders + have an alumni mentor network. Get on a 1:1 with a healthcare-track mentor.
- **Yale Entrepreneurial Institute (YEI)** (yei.yale.edu) — runs the Startup Yale accelerator. Even if you don't apply, their staff have networks worth tapping.
- **Yale School of Management Healthcare Club** — has a directory of alumni in healthcare leadership. Many run or advise practices.

### Faculty + classmates as channels

- **Yale School of Public Health** — faculty in health policy + management know practice owners in CT.
- **Yale School of Medicine** — there are clinician-faculty who do private psychiatric practice on the side. Office hours, cold email, walk-in. They'll either be your customer or know one.
- **Specific faculty to consider** (find current emails on yale.edu): anyone teaching health-care management, RCM, or "healthcare operations." Even if they don't bite, ask them for 2 names.
- **Yale alumni** in healthcare administration via Cross-Campus (career services) or the alumni directory. Filter by "healthcare administration" / "practice management" / "RCM."

### Yale events worth attending in person

- **Yale Healthcare Conference** (annual, usually April) — practice managers + RCM executives attend. Volunteer to staff it, you get badge access.
- Any healthcare-themed startup Tuesday or pitch event at Tsai CITY.

### The Yale email move

Send the cold email from your Yale `@yale.edu` address, not a Gmail. Spam
filters trust it. Subject lines that mention Yale clear inboxes that other
subject lines don't. Don't bury the lede — put "Yale student" in the first
sentence.

## Tier 2: free public databases

- **CMS NPPES NPI Registry** — every licensed provider in the US, free, public, searchable: <https://npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov/search>
  - Search filter: Provider Type = "Mental Health Counselor" or "Psychologist" or "Physical Therapist", state CT, city New Haven.
  - Gives you NPI, practice name, address. Doesn't give you email — but a 5-second Google "practice name + email" usually does, or you can find the practice's website and use a generic "info@" or the practice manager listed on the About page.

- **Connecticut DPH Provider Search** — state-licensed providers in CT specifically. Easier to filter for active independent practices.

- **Connecticut Behavioral Health Partnership directory** — state's contracted behavioral-health providers. Most are independent. <https://www.ctbhp.com>

- **Yelp + Google Maps** — search "psychotherapist" / "physical therapy" + ZIP code. The ones with multiple Google reviews and a real website (not Psychology Today only) are exactly your ICP.

- **Psychology Today therapist directory** — every therapist with a website. Filter by location.

## Tier 3: paid databases (don't pay until tier 1+2 are exhausted)

- **Definitive Healthcare** — single best source for practice info, but $$$. Yale may have a license through the SOM or the library; check first.
- **Carevoyance** — affordable, sales-oriented.
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — $80/mo, 1-month free trial. Filter: "Practice Manager" OR "Owner" + healthcare industry + Connecticut + 11-50 employees. Gives you names + LinkedIn profiles, you still need the email. Use Hunter.io free tier (50 emails/mo) to find emails.

## Tier 4: directly walk in (New Haven only)

This is the move 90% of founders skip because they're scared. Don't skip it.

Pick 5 independent behavioral-health practices in New Haven on a Monday or
Wednesday afternoon. Walk in, ask the receptionist if the owner or practice
manager is around, hand them a one-pager (write one based on `icp.md` and
the master plan). Worst case you leave a card. Best case you have a meeting
that day.

5 walk-ins ≈ 1-2 hour timebox in a single afternoon. The conversion rate is
genuinely 5-10× cold email. New Haven is small enough that this works.

## Search-query starter pack

Paste these into Google + LinkedIn:

```
site:linkedin.com/in/ ("practice manager" OR "office manager") "psychiatric" Connecticut
site:linkedin.com/in/ ("practice administrator" OR "director of operations") "physical therapy" New Haven
"behavioral health" "private practice" New Haven
"physical therapy" "independently owned" Connecticut
"practice manager" psychiatric "Athena" OR "DrChrono" OR "SimplePractice"
```
