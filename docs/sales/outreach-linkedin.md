# LinkedIn DM templates

Use these for leads where you have a LinkedIn URL but not a confirmed email.
LinkedIn DMs have a lower reply rate than cold email (3-7% vs 8-15%) but are
the right channel when the only thing you have is a profile.

## DM Variant A — connect-with-note + immediate ask (recommended)

Connection request (max 300 characters):

> Hi [First Name] — I'm a Yale student building Overturn, software that
> automates denial appeals for small behavioral-health practices. Trying
> to learn how teams like yours handle denials today. Would love to
> connect and ask 1-2 questions if you're open to it.

After they accept:

> Thanks for connecting, [First Name]. As I mentioned — I'm building
> Overturn at Yale, AI agents that draft and submit appeals on denied
> claims (outcomes-based pricing, you pay nothing if we don't recover).
>
> Before I get into whether it'd help you, I want to learn how
> [Practice Name] handles denial follow-up today. Would you have 15
> minutes for a research call next week? Some times: [3 options].

## DM Variant B — if you've already shared a connection or had a touchpoint

> Hi [First Name] — quick note. [Mutual / context: "I saw your post about
> insurance documentation pain", "[Mutual] suggested I reach out", "We're
> both in [Yale group]"]. I'm building Overturn — AI denial appeals for
> small specialty practices, outcomes-based pricing. Would you have 15
> minutes for a research call this/next week? Trying to understand the
> problem from people who live it.

## What works on LinkedIn

- **Always include a note** with connection requests. Naked requests = ignored or rejected.
- **Be brief**. LinkedIn cuts off long messages; the first 100 characters matter most.
- **Mention Yale early**. Same logic as cold email.
- **Don't include a link**. LinkedIn deprioritizes messages with URLs.
- **Send Monday morning** — highest reply rate.

## What doesn't work on LinkedIn

- Pitching in the connection request itself ("Hi, want to save 30% on RCM?").
- Long voice notes.
- Sending a deck.
- "Pick your brain" framing.

## Follow-up

- **Day 0**: Connect with note.
- **If accepted but no reply by day 4**: Short re-ping ("Hi [name], wanted to bump this — any chance for that 15 min?").
- **If no reply by day 10**: Move on. Maybe re-engage in a quarter with a value-add (an article, an industry stat, a new product update).
- **If declined connection**: That's a no. Don't try again from another account.

## When to switch to email

If you find a LinkedIn lead that doesn't accept your request within a week,
look for their email (Hunter.io, ZoomInfo trial, practice website "Contact"
page). Cold email at that point usually outperforms re-requesting on LinkedIn.
