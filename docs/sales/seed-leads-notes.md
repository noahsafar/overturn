# Seed leads — notes

I pulled the first 7 rows of `leads.csv` from a public web search of behavioral
health and physical therapy practices in New Haven / surrounding CT. These are
real, publicly-listed practices that *look like* ICP fits at a glance.

**Do not blast them all on day 1.** Vet each one in 5 minutes before you reach out:
- Confirm provider count via the practice's "Our Team" page.
- Confirm they're independent (not affiliated with Yale New Haven Health or
  Hartford HealthCare).
- Find the owner / practice manager on LinkedIn.
- Find a real email (Hunter.io, the practice's Contact page, or info@ as last resort).

## Practices I deliberately did NOT add (anti-persona matches from the search)

These came up in the same searches but disqualify per the ICP doc:

- **Yale New Haven Psychiatry and Behavioral Health Services** — owned by Yale
  New Haven Health System (5 hospitals + Northeast Medical Group, hundreds of
  hospital-employed physicians). Hospital-system procurement gauntlet. Skip.
- **Clifford Beers** — nonprofit community mental health agency, federally
  qualified. Different funding model, different denial profile, harder sell.
  Skip for Phase 1.
- **Hartford HealthCare Rehabilitation Network** — 30+ locations all owned by
  Hartford HealthCare hospital system. Same problem. Skip.
- **FYZICAL** — franchise network, not independent. Their corporate office
  handles RCM. Skip.

## Where to find 23 more (to hit your 30-lead target)

For behavioral health:
- CT Behavioral Health Partnership directory: <https://www.ctbhp.com>
- Psychology Today CT directory, filtered to group practices with their own websites
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator: "Practice manager" OR "Owner" + Connecticut + Behavioral Health, 5-50 employees

For physical therapy:
- American Physical Therapy Association CT chapter member directory
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator: "Owner" + "Physical Therapy" + Connecticut, independent

Same approach for Boston / NYC metros once you've worked through CT.

## What I couldn't get from a web search

- **Owner names / practice manager names**. Many of these need LinkedIn or
  the practice's "About" page to find a real human.
- **EHR / billing-co**. Practices don't advertise these. You'll learn on the
  discovery call.
- **Denial volume estimate**. Same — discovery-call data.

## How long this should take you

- 90 minutes to vet these 7 + find 23 more
- 30 minutes to find owner/PM names for the 20 most promising
- 30 minutes to write personalized first lines on the top 15

Total: ~2.5 hours of research before sending a single email. Skip this and your
reply rate halves.
