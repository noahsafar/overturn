# Follow-up email templates

Five templates. Use the one that matches where the conversation is.

---

## F1 — Day 4 cold-email follow-up (no reply yet)

**Subject**: re: [previous subject]

> Hi [First Name] —
>
> Quick bump in case the previous note got buried. Still hoping to learn
> how [Practice Name] handles denial follow-up — 15 minutes, no pitch.
>
> [3 specific time options].
>
> Noah

(That's it. Two sentences. Anything longer reads as desperate.)

---

## F2 — Day 11 cold-email final touch (still no reply)

**Subject**: closing the loop on Overturn

> Hi [First Name] —
>
> Last note from me. I'll stop reaching out after this one. Two things
> in case they're useful:
>
> 1. If timing's just bad, no need to reply — I'll re-ping in a quarter
>    with anything new we've learned.
>
> 2. If you know anyone running an independent behavioral-health practice
>    in CT who'd be curious — I'd be grateful for an intro. Easy reply
>    is just a name.
>
> Either way, thanks for your time and good luck this quarter.
>
> Noah

(The "ask for an intro instead" move converts ~5% of dead leads into a warm
intro to a different lead. Worth it.)

## F3 — Post-discovery-call same-day summary

**Subject**: Notes from our call — [Practice Name]

> Hi [First Name] —
>
> Thanks for the time today. Wanted to send a quick summary so I know
> I heard you right, and so you have something to point your team at if
> they want context.
>
> Here's what I heard:
>
> - **What you do today**: [2-3 sentences capturing their denial process —
>   who handles it, what volume, what gets worked vs. written off]
>
> - **Where it hurts**: [the specific pain they named, in their words. e.g.
>   "Your biller spends a full day per BCBS CO-50 appeal, and most of the
>   <$200 denials get written off because the math doesn't work."]
>
> - **What you said you'd want from a tool**: [their words again — e.g.,
>   "Drafted letter you review in 5 minutes, not 60. Citation checking
>   so you're not exposed to bad references. Pricing that doesn't add
>   a monthly bill if it doesn't recover money."]
>
> Did I get that right? Reply with any corrections.
>
> Next step we agreed: [the thing you said you'd do — "I'll send a
> 2-page pilot agreement draft by [date]" or "follow up call on
> [date]"].
>
> Thanks,
> Noah

(This email does three things at once: shows you listened, gives them a
referenceable artifact, locks in the next step. Send within 60 minutes of
the call — speed is signal.)

## F4 — Pilot pitch (after a positive discovery + a follow-up call)

**Subject**: 2-page pilot proposal for [Practice Name]

> Hi [First Name] —
>
> As promised, attached is the pilot agreement draft for [Practice Name].
> The short version:
>
> - **Term**: 60 days, starting whenever you give us SFTP access to your
>   clearinghouse so the denials can flow in.
> - **Scope**: We work every denied claim that comes through during the
>   pilot window. Your reviewer approves every appeal before it goes out.
> - **Pricing**: 25% of dollars recovered. Zero if we recover nothing.
> - **Term-out**: Either side can end the pilot with 7 days notice for
>   any reason. If you want to continue at 60 days, no new contract —
>   we just extend month-to-month at the same terms.
>
> Two things I'd ask:
>
> 1. Have your attorney look at the draft (the BAA is the most important
>    part — make sure they're comfortable with how we handle PHI). Happy
>    to schedule a call with my [attorney TBD] if useful.
>
> 2. Loop me in to whichever person at your practice would be the
>    day-one reviewer of drafts. The pilot lives or dies on that person's
>    speed of approval in week 1.
>
> Realistically, what's your timeline to a yes or no?
>
> Noah

## What to send WITH each template

- **F1 / F2**: nothing — terse is the point.
- **F3 (post-call summary)**: nothing yet. The summary IS the artifact.
- **F4 (pilot pitch)**: `pilot-agreement-draft.md` (your attorney-reviewed version) + a one-page case for the practice.
- **F5 (re-engagement)**: nothing — same logic as F1.

## Speed-of-response benchmarks

- **First reply to a discovery-call request**: within 2 business hours, ideally same day.
- **Post-call summary**: within 60 minutes of hanging up.
- **Pilot agreement after they ask for one**: within 24 hours.

These benchmarks are the difference between "I'll send some info" leads
converting at 5% vs 30%.
